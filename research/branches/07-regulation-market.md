# Regulation, market figures, what the jury wants

Research date: 2026-10-23. "Unverified" marks a fact read only in a secondary source or recalled, not checked in the primary text today.

## Key facts (6 bullets max, each with a source link and a date)

- **France: manipulation fraud is the problem, fake bank advisor its main form.** OSMP 2025 report (press release, 7 Sep 2026): manipulation fraud reached EUR 516m in 2025 (+34% in a year), 41.6% of all payment fraud; "the most frequent form is the fake bank advisor fraud", mostly by credit transfer (EUR 376m), while card fraud hit its lowest rate ever (EUR 47 per EUR 100,000). Total payment fraud about EUR 1.24bn (+3.8%), figure from secondary coverage, consistent with 516 / 41.6%. The OSMP's 4 priorities: telecoms (spoofed numbers, a single anti-fraud number), digital platforms, bank tools "including AI and deeper knowledge of the customer and the devices used", and awareness ("a bank never needs its customer to do anything to stop a suspicious payment"). The press release does not say how many cases use a cloned voice: no public figure found. Sources: https://www.banque-france.fr/system/files/2026-09/Communique_de_presse_du_rapport_2025.pdf , https://www.lafinancepourtous.com/2026/09/14/moyens-de-paiement-les-fraudes-par-manipulation-en-forte-hausse/
- **EU: liability moves to the bank (PSD3 / PSR).** Provisional deal, 27 Nov 2025: when a scammer "pretends to be a PSP employee and tricks the customer into approving a payment", the PSP "must refund the full amount" provided the customer reports it to the police and to the PSP; PSPs must check that payee name and identifier match; PSPs are liable when they lack appropriate fraud prevention; online platforms that leave reported fraud content up are liable to the refunding PSP. Formal adoption and application date: not confirmed today (unverified). Scope dispute: Finologee reads it as impersonation of the customer's own PSP only, BioCatch as banks or authorities. Sources: https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/en/press-room/20251121IPR31540/payment-services-deal-more-protection-from-online-fraud-and-hidden-fees (27 Nov 2025), https://finologee.com/psr-what-the-provisional-political-agreement-signals-for-psps/
- **UK precedent: mandatory APP fraud reimbursement since 7 Oct 2024.** First year (to 30 Sep 2025): 88% of money lost (GBP 173m) reimbursed, about 269,000 claims. Per-claim cap GBP 85,000, cost split between sending and receiving PSP (unverified, recalled). Source: https://www.psr.org.uk/information-for-consumers/app-scams-reimbursement-dashboard (updated 30 Jan 2026)
- **AI Act: deepfake transparency in force, 1:1 verification not high-risk.** Article 50 (disclosure of AI interaction, labelling of deepfakes by deployers) applies since 2 Aug 2026; the Digital Omnibus only gave providers of generative systems already on the market until 2 Dec 2026 for machine-readable marking (Art. 50(2)); sources disagree on whether the Omnibus is formally adopted. Annex III point 1 excludes "biometric verification the sole purpose of which is to confirm that a specific natural person is the person he or she claims to be", and point 5(b) excludes "AI systems used for the purpose of detecting financial fraud" from high-risk. Sources: https://www.ictrecht.nl/en/blog/heads-up-ai-act-transparency-obligations-now-in-effect (Aug 2026), https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-eu-ai-act-article-50-transparency-20260729/ (29 Jul 2026), https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/annex/3/
- **eIDAS 2.0: the EU Digital Identity Wallet becomes a bank channel.** Member States must offer a certified wallet by end 2026; banks and PSPs that require strong customer authentication must accept it for onboarding and SCA by 24 Dec 2027 (secondary sources, not checked in Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 itself: unverified date). Source: https://fintech.global/2026/09/04/what-banks-need-to-know-before-the-eudi-wallet-deadline/ (4 Sep 2026)
- **Biometrics are sensitive data (GDPR art. 9), and CNIL wants them under the user's control.** CNIL principles for biometrics used by individuals: the template stays on the person's device, or is stored "unusable without the person's intervention"; consent must be free and specific with a non-biometric alternative offered at no cost; a DPIA justifies the need. DORA (applies since 17 Jan 2025) puts any detection vendor in the bank's ICT third-party register (date recalled, not checked today). Source: https://cnil.fr/fr/biometrie-disposition-de-particuliers-quels-sont-les-principes-respecter (10 Apr 2018, still the reference page; no 2025 or 2026 CNIL guidance on voice authentication in banks found)

## Five facts for the pitch slide (each with its source)

1. **EUR 516m** stolen by manipulation fraud in France in 2025, **+34% in one year**, now **41.6%** of all payment fraud; fake bank advisor is the most frequent form. Banque de France, OSMP 2025 report, 7 Sep 2026: https://www.banque-france.fr/system/files/2026-09/Communique_de_presse_du_rapport_2025.pdf
2. **The bank pays now**: under the EU payments deal, a bank must refund the full amount when a fraudster poses as its employee. European Parliament, 27 Nov 2025: https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/en/press-room/20251121IPR31540/payment-services-deal-more-protection-from-online-fraud-and-hidden-fees
3. **88% (GBP 173m)** of APP scam losses refunded in the UK's first year of mandatory reimbursement: what EU banks will face. PSR dashboard, Oct 2024 to Sep 2025: https://www.psr.org.uk/information-for-consumers/app-scams-reimbursement-dashboard
4. **1 in 5 biometric fraud attempts is a deepfake**; deepfaked selfies **+58%** and injection attacks **+40%** in 2025. Entrust 2026 Identity Fraud Report, 18 Nov 2025: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251118135338/en/Deepfakes-Social-Engineering-and-Injection-Attacks-on-the-Rise-Entrust-2026-Identity-Fraud-Report-Reveals-Surging-Attacks-and-Diversifying-Tactics
5. **Deepfakes are 1 in 15 fraud attempts** in European finance (6.5%, from 0.1% three years before), yet **only 22%** of institutions use AI-based fraud prevention. Signicat, "The Battle Against AI-Driven Identity Fraud", Feb 2025: https://www.logisticsit.com/articles/2025/02/25/fraud-attempts-with-deepfakes-have-increased-by-2137-over-the-past-three-years (the headline "+2137%" does not match 0.1% to 6.5%: quote the shares, not the percentage)

Backups: Sumsub 2025-2026 report (Nov 2025): multi-step "sophisticated" fraud up 180% (10% to 28% of identity fraud), deepfake fraud in France +96% (France figure via CB News only, unverified): https://sumsub.com/identity-fraud-report-2025 , https://www.cbnews.fr/etudes/les-fraudes-ia-sont-hausse-sumsub . ## Verdict (2 or 3 lines: which problem a finance jury will find the most credible)

The fake bank advisor call is the one: a French official figure (EUR 516m, +34%, Banque de France, Sep 2026), a cost the bank now carries (PSR refund), and a regulator asking for AI. Pitch a check that proves "is this really my bank calling?" in the app and holds a transfer made during a suspicious call, with no voiceprint stored centrally (CNIL), and say plainly that the share of cloned voices in these calls is not measured yet.
