# Zero-knowledge identity from real ID documents

## Key facts (6 bullets max, each with a source link and a date)

- **Banks will have to accept the EU wallet.** eIDAS 2.0 (Regulation (EU) 2024/1183, in force May 2024): each member state must offer a certified EUDI Wallet by end of 2026, and banks, insurers and payment providers must accept it for onboarding and strong customer authentication by 24 Dec 2027, only micro-enterprises exempt ([fintech.global, 2026-09-04](https://fintech.global/2026/09/04/what-banks-need-to-know-before-the-eudi-wallet-deadline/)). The exact wallet date differs between secondary sources (24 Dec, 31 Dec, Nov 2026): unverified against the regulation text. The wallet covers identification only: sanctions and PEP screening stay the bank's job ([NameScan, 2025/2026](https://namescan.io/insights/eidas-2-eudi-wallet-aml-onboarding/)).
- **France Identité already runs an open developer sandbox.** The "EUDIW Unfold" playground plugs wallets, issuers and verifiers into France Identité plus German, Austrian, Dutch, Polish sandboxes and the EU reference implementation, 35 partners in 16 countries. The test app "Partenaires" is requested through a Firebase App Distribution (Android) or TestFlight (iOS) form, loaded with a certified test identity, and issues a PID and an age-verification credential in ISO mdoc; hosted verifiers cover OpenID4VP 1.0, the Digital Credentials API (Annex C), mdoc URI (Annex A), ISO 18013-5 proximity and the EU `av://` age flow ([France Identité quickstart](https://playground.france-identite.gouv.fr/marketplace/wallets/fin_quickstart/), read 2026-10-23; a partner page there is dated May 2026). Approval delay for the test app: unverified.
- **Self (self.xyz) turns an NFC passport into a ZK proof, and has a mock mode.** Verifier rules: `minimumAge`, `excludedCountries` (max 40), `ofac`; optional disclosures: name, nationality, gender, date of birth, passport number, expiry, issuing state ([Self disclosures](https://docs.self.xyz/docs/self-pass/disclosures/), read 2026-10-23). Supported now: ICAO e-passports (60+ countries), Aadhaar, partner KYC attestations; NFC phone (most iPhones, Android 8+) ([supported documents](https://docs.self.xyz/docs/self-enterprise/flows/supported-documents/)). The open-source "Self Pass" SDK is now labelled legacy in favour of "Self Enterprise" (credits; Free tier = one-time signup grant, test traffic not billed) ([plans](https://docs.self.xyz/docs/self-enterprise/billing/plans/)). Mock passports are generated in the Self app with chosen nationality, age, expiry and OFAC status ([mock passports](https://docs.self.xyz/docs/self-enterprise/guides/using-mock-passports/)). EU ID cards: claimed in 2025 sources, not on the current supported-documents page: unverified.
- **zkPassport is the no-account alternative, now owned by Aztec.** `@zkpassport/sdk`: `new ZKPassport("your.domain")`, query builder `gte("age", 18)`, `in/out("nationality", ...)`, `disclose(...)`, QR `url`, then server-side `verify()` (local or API mode); it returns a `uniqueIdentifier` stable per document, domain and scope, usable as a privacy-preserving account key. README: "multiple internal audits, no external audit yet" ([SDK README](https://github.com/zkpassport/zkpassport-packages/tree/main/packages/zkpassport-sdk), read 2026-10-23). Aztec Labs acquired Obsidion (zkPassport's company) on 2026-05-27 ([SiliconANGLE, 2026-05-27](https://siliconangle.com/?p=763562)). Mock or dev documents: not found, unverified. Rarimo (contracts marked "experimental") and Anon Aadhaar (PSE, JS and Solidity SDK, India only) are precedents, not 20 h material ([Anon Aadhaar](https://pse.dev/en/projects/anon-aadhaar)).
- **The selective-disclosure standards are final.** SD-JWT is RFC 9901, Nov 2025 ([RFC Editor](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9901.html)); OpenID4VP 1.0 is what the French and EU sandboxes speak (above); W3C VC 2.0 became a Recommendation in May 2025 (secondary source only, unverified on w3.org: [BlockEden forum](https://blockeden.xyz/forum/t/w3c-vc-2-0-is-now-a-standard-what-developers-need-to-know/335)). BBS signatures status: not checked, unverified. Google open-sourced Longfellow ZK (ZK proofs over ISO mdoc, for "over 18" without the birth date) on 2025-07-03, and the EU age-verification spec plans it as an experimental feature ([GIGAZINE, 2025-07-04](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20250704-google-zero-knowledge-proof), [IETF 124 CFRG slides, Nov 2025](https://datatracker.ietf.org/meeting/124/materials/slides-124-cfrg-longfellow-zk-01)).
- **Why it answers deepfakes: selfie KYC is the attacked surface.** iProov 2025: native virtual camera attacks +2,665% YoY, face swaps +300%; Entrust 2026: injection attacks +40% YoY, deepfake selfies +58% in 2025 (vendor figures, [Infosecurity Magazine](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/deepfake-identity-attack-every/), [hstoday](https://www.hstoday.us/subject-matter-areas/cybersecurity/surge-in-digital-injection-and-deepfake-attacks-on-identity-verification-systems/)). A chip proof rests on the issuing state's signature, which no generative model can forge; its blind spot is a stolen document (no face match unless added).

## Project ideas (2 or 3: one-line pitch, the demo moment the jury sees, the stack, the main risk)

1. **"Prove it's you" button against voice-deepfake payment fraud.** Pitch: a cloned voice can order a wire transfer, it cannot sign with a passport chip; the bank operator challenges any voice or video instruction with a ZK proof bound to that transaction and to the client enrolled at onboarding, without receiving any personal data.
   - Demo moment: a cloned "CFO" voice (pre-recorded) asks for a 250 k EUR transfer and sounds real; the operator clicks "Challenge"; the real person taps the passport on a phone and the console turns green in under a minute (same `uniqueIdentifier` as at enrolment, proof bound to the transaction ID); the impostor has nothing to show.
   - Stack: Next.js back-office page, `@zkpassport/sdk` (or Self with mock passports for rehearsal), SQLite with one `uniqueIdentifier` per client, an LLM that scores the incoming instruction's risk and decides when to challenge.
   - Main risk: binding the proof to a per-transaction nonce (zkPassport `scope` changes the identifier; Self `userDefinedData` looks like the slot): check this in the first hour. Second risk: a real NFC passport and the phone app working on the venue network.

2. **Zero-data onboarding: KYC that stores proofs, not passports.** Pitch: an asset manager onboards a client with "over 18, not from an excluded country, not sanctioned" proven from the passport chip or the EUDI / France Identité wallet, and stores no document scan to leak, ready for the Dec 2027 obligation.
   - Demo moment: side by side, a classic flow accepts an injected deepfake selfie (pre-recorded), while the ZK flow has no camera to attack; then the "what the bank stores" panel: booleans and a proof, zero PII, next to the classic flow's folder of scans.
   - Stack: Self Enterprise test mode (mock passports, no real document needed) plus the France Identité hosted verifier if the test app is approved; Next.js; an LLM copilot listing which CDD requirements the proof covers and which it does not (PEP, address, EU sanctions).
   - Main risk: the jury knows Self's OFAC check is US-only and the wallet does not replace AML screening: say it before they do.

3. **Human-backed AI agent mandate (stretch).** Pitch: a client's AI trading or payment agent carries a credential saying "acts for one verified adult EU resident, limit X", issued after a ZK passport proof; the bank accepts the agent's orders without seeing who the client is.
   - Demo moment: two agents place the same order; the one with the mandate passes, the rogue one (no proof behind it) is rejected with the reason.
   - Stack: zkPassport or Self, an SD-JWT VC issued by the team's backend (library not checked, unverified), an LLM agent.
   - Main risk: three moving parts in 20 h; only pick it if ideas 1 or 2 are done by midnight.

## Verdict (2 or 3 lines)

A ZK proof from the ID chip is the one answer to deepfakes in this theme that a 4-person team can demo live in 20 h: trust comes from the state's signature, not from a face or a voice an AI can fake, and no personal data reaches the bank. Build idea 1 on zkPassport (no account) with Self mock passports as the rehearsal fallback, and use France Identité / EUDI and the Dec 2027 obligation as the "why banks will need this" slide, not as the critical path.
