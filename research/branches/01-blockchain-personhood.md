# Blockchain and proof of personhood

## Key facts (6 bullets max, each with a source link and a date)

- **Biometric proof of personhood is a regulatory minefield in Europe and beyond.** On 5 May 2025 the Kenyan High Court ordered World (ex Worldcoin) to delete all iris and face data within 7 days and stop collecting, ruling that consent bought with crypto tokens was invalid ([The Block, 2025-05-06](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2025-05-06-kenya-court-rules-against-sam-altmans-world-orders-data-deletion-and-halts-biometrics-collection-353182)). Before that, Spain's AEPD suspended it (2024) and Bavaria's BayLDA found GDPR violations and ordered deletion ([Decrypt, Dec 2024](https://decrypt.co/298090/german-watchdog-cracks-down-on-worldcoin-over-biometric-data)). Bans in Brazil, South Korea and Indonesia appear in secondary sources only: unverified.
- **The EDPB says: no personal data on chain, and a hash is still personal data.** Guidelines 02/2025 on blockchain, version 2.0 adopted 7 July 2026: on-chain erasure may be technically impossible, so store data off-chain and put only a cryptographic commitment on chain; a salted or keyed hash of personal data stays personal data; erasure means deleting the off-chain data and the key so the on-chain value becomes unlinkable ([EDPB PDF, 2026-07-07](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2026-07/edpb_guidelines_202502_blockchain_v2_en.pdf)). A public key tied to a person is personal data too.
- **Proof-of-personhood landscape is consolidating, not booming.** Holonym bought Gitcoin Passport on 10 Feb 2025 and renamed it Human Passport (2.1M+ users, 34.5M ZK credentials claimed) ([Decrypt, 2025-02-10](https://decrypt.co/305249/holonym-foundation-acquires-gitcoin-passport-to-onboard-its-2m-users-to-create-worlds-largest-proof-of-humanity-solution), [human.tech 2025 wrap-up](https://human.tech/blog/holonym-foundation-2025-wrapped)). World's verified count is reported anywhere from 17M (Oct 2025, a WLD-holding company) to "nearly 38M users" (Jan 2026, secondary): unverified, no primary figure found. Humanity Protocol (palm veins, hash on chain, Polygon CDK L2) opened a testnet beta in May 2025 ([CoinEx Academy, 2025](https://www.coinex.com/en/academy/detail/2641-what-is-humanity-protocol-decentralized-identity-with-palm-biometrics-and-zero-knowledge-proofs)); mainnet date and user count unverified. BrightID and Proof of Humanity: not checked this session.
- **The new angle is "a human behind the AI agent".** World released AgentKit in March 2026 to prove a verified human stands behind an AI shopping agent ([Outlook Business citing TechCrunch, March 2026](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/deeptech/openais-world-project-launches-tool-to-distinguish-people-from-ai-agents)), while Tools for Humanity reportedly cut staff as revenue lagged ([Mobile ID World, 2026](https://mobileidworld.com/tools-for-humanity-reportedly-conducting-layoffs-as-world-app-revenue-lags/), date unverified).
- **The finance threat is real and is about authorisation, not existence.** Arup lost HK$200M (about US$25.6M) in 15 transfers after a Hong Kong employee joined a video call where the CFO and every colleague were deepfakes ([CNN via ABC17, 2024-05-16](https://abc17news.com/money/cnn-business-consumer/2024/05/16/british-engineering-giant-arup-revealed-as-25-million-deepfake-scam-victim/)). No blockchain identity of "the CFO exists" would have helped; a signature the call could not fake would have.
- **Provenance beats detection, and its weak spot is where a chain helps.** CISA endorsed C2PA Content Credentials in January 2025, but the signed manifest lives inside the file and is stripped by re-uploads and conversions ([SoftwareSeni, 2025](https://www.softwareseni.com/how-c2pa-content-credentials-work-and-what-their-limits-are/)); an external hash registry (on chain or not) lets a stripped file still be looked up. The EU wallet (EUDI) must be issued by every member state by end 2026 and accepted by banks about a year later, over OpenID4VP 1.0 (final 10 July 2025) ([DPS, 2026](https://dps.de/en/news/eudi-wallet-an-infrastructure-issue-for-banks/), [Didit](https://didit.me/de/blog/openid4vp-verifier)); exact dates differ between sources.

## Project ideas (2 or 3: one-line pitch, the demo moment the jury sees, the stack, the main risk)

1. **"Signed or it didn't happen": deepfake-proof payment approvals (best fit).**
   - Pitch: a voice or a face on a call is no longer an identity; a payment order is approved only by a signature from the executive's phone key, whose status sits in a public, revocable attestation registry shared by the company, its bank and its auditor.
   - Demo moment: replay of the Arup case. A deepfake CFO (pre-recorded clip or live voice clone from another branch) asks for an urgent transfer; the bank's screen shows "no signature from the CFO key" in red. The real CFO taps Face ID on his phone, the order (IBAN, amount, nonce hashed) turns green with a link to the attestation on easscan. Then the CFO's phone is "stolen": one revocation, and the next signature is refused everywhere at once.
   - Stack: Next.js, SimpleWebAuthn, EAS SDK on Base Sepolia (off-chain attestations as fallback), viem.
   - Main risk: the jury asks "why not the bank's own PKI or a signed database?". Answer ready: several parties who do not trust one operator, a public timestamped revocation list, no vendor lock-in. Enrollment (who gets the key) stays the weak link: say it is done through existing KYC, later the EUDI wallet.

2. **Anchored voice: provenance registry for official communications.**
   - Pitch: every official audio or video of an executive or advisor (earnings call, client message) is C2PA-signed and its fingerprint anchored with a timestamp; anyone can drop a clip and see "issued by X at T" or "unknown origin".
   - Demo moment: two clips of the same CEO, one real, one cloned; the real one is found in the registry even after a re-upload stripped its metadata, the fake is not.
   - Stack: c2patool, a perceptual audio fingerprint (Chromaprint) or frame hash, EAS attestation per Merkle root of a batch, a small upload page.
   - Main risk: re-encoding changes exact hashes, so lookup needs fuzzy fingerprints with false matches; and "not anchored" proves absence of provenance, not that the clip is fake.

3. **One human, one account, zero identity shared: anti synthetic-identity onboarding.**
   - Pitch: after KYC, the person joins a Semaphore group (or uses World ID); each bank gets a per-bank nullifier, so a deepfaked or synthetic applicant cannot open a second account, and the bank never learns more than "unique, verified human".
   - Demo moment: the same person tries to open two accounts, the second is refused, and the chain shows only a Merkle root, nothing personal.
   - Stack: Semaphore v4 or IDKit + simulator, a contract holding the group root, Next.js.
   - Main risk: zero-knowledge setup time eats the 20 h; World ID brings its biometric and regulatory baggage in front of a finance jury.

## Verdict (2 or 3 lines: is the blockchain needed, and what for)

A chain cannot prove that a person exists: it binds a key, and enrollment (KYC, EUDI wallet) does the real work; storing identity on chain also fights the EDPB's 2026 guidelines. It earns its place only as a shared, timestamped, operator-free registry of attestations, revocations and content hashes between parties who do not trust each other. Pitch idea 1 with the chain as the back end, nothing personal on chain.
