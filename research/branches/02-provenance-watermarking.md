# Content provenance and watermarking

Scouted 2026-10-23. "Unverified" marks a fact seen only in a secondary source or not checked in this session. Setup times are my estimates for a team of 4, not measured.

## Key facts (6 bullets max, each with a source link and a date)

- **C2PA is at version 2.4** (spec dated 2026-04-15, release listed 2026-04-21); 2.3 (Dec 2025 / Jan 2026) added live video signing at the CMAF segment level; 2.4 reportedly adds plain text, HTML embedding and an AI disclosure assertion (unverified, secondary source). The conformance program opened 2025-06-04 and lists 54 products from 31 companies by 2026-05-14 (unverified). Sources: [Wikipedia, Content Credentials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Content_Credentials), [C2PA 2.3 spec PDF, 2026-01-05](https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.3/specs/_attachments/C2PA_Specification.pdf), [softwareseni, 2026](https://softwareseni.com/c2pa-adoption-in-2026-hardware-platforms-and-verification-reality).
- **Who signs at capture, who displays it.** Pixel 10 Pixel Camera signs every photo, C2PA 2.2, Assurance Level 2 via Tensor G5 + Titan M2, offline timestamps ([Google Security Blog, 2025-09](https://security.googleblog.com/2025/09/pixel-android-trusted-images-c2pa-content-credentials.html)). Samsung Galaxy S23+ (Photo Assist, Jan 2025) signs only AI-edited images, interim trust list, not conformant (unverified against Samsung). Cameras: Sony, Leica, Canon (R1, R5 II, 2025-07); Nikon suspended its service and revoked certificates after a vulnerability. Display: LinkedIn (2024-05), TikTok, Meta, YouTube, Google Search, Pinterest (2025-04), X (2026-01); Cloudflare and Fastly preserve credentials in transit ([Wikipedia, read 2026-10-23](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Content_Credentials)).
- **Stripping is the default.** Most social platforms drop embedded metadata on upload; in a Washington Post test (Oct 2025) no platform kept Content Credentials on a video (reported second hand, unverified against the Post). A screenshot or re-encode removes the manifest. Durable credentials pair the manifest with an invisible watermark and a fingerprint so it can be recovered ([softwareseni, 2026](https://softwareseni.com/durable-content-credentials-how-provenance-survives-metadata-stripping)).
- **C2PA itself was broken twice in 2026 by David Buchanan.** 2026-08-25: root on a Pixel 8a/9a (CVE-2026-43499 or fault injection) lets an attacker sign arbitrary images as genuine Pixel Camera captures; Google closed the hardware part as "won't fix" ([blog](https://www.da.vidbuchanan.co.uk/blog/android-c2pa.html)). 2026-10-02: a signer can exclude the whole file from the hash ("exclusions"), so the image can be edited and signatures plus RFC 3161 timestamps still verify; validators he checked do not flag it, no fix yet ([blog](https://www.da.vidbuchanan.co.uk/blog/hacking-time.html)). Lesson: a verifier must reject broad exclusions and trust only its own list of signers.
- **SynthID and OpenAI.** The SynthID Detector portal opened to the public 2026-10-06: image, video, audio (not text), reads Google, OpenAI, Nvidia and Kakao marks, no public API, automated or bulk use banned by its terms ([ppc.land, Oct 2026](https://ppc.land/googles-synthid-detector-gains-public-access-and-covers-4-companies/)). SynthID Text is open source in Hugging Face Transformers v4.46 (2024-10-23), weak after thorough rewrite or translation ([HF blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/synthid-text)). OpenAI adds C2PA plus SynthID to images and previews a verify tool (May 2026, [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/advancing-content-provenance)); Sora 2 videos are said to carry C2PA, but one user test found none on non-Pro downloads (unverified, [EA Forum](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/oMJzXc79CexLqi52F/openai-does-not-appear-to-be-applying-watermarks-honestly)).
- **Watermarks are removable, and the open ones are free.** UnMarker (USENIX Security 2025) drops SynthID image detection from about 100% to about 21% without detector access; Google disputes the figure ([The Register, 2025-07-24](https://www.theregister.com/2025/07/24/ai_watermarks_unmarker/), [code](https://github.com/andrekassis/ai-watermark)). Meta AudioSeal (MIT, v0.2 with streaming 2024-12-12, [GitHub](https://github.com/facebookresearch/audioseal)) and Video Seal (MIT, Dec 2024; PixelSeal and ChunkySeal Dec 2025, [GitHub](https://github.com/facebookresearch/videoseal)) are open source.

## What is buildable in 20 h (tools, APIs, models, libraries with links, free tier or not, setup time)

| Tool | What it gives us | Cost | Setup (est.) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [c2pa-python](https://github.com/contentauth/c2pa-python) (`pip install c2pa-python`) | Read, validate, create, sign and embed manifests in Python | Free, open source | 1 to 2 h | Python 3.10+, prebuilt Rust wheels for macOS/Linux/Windows. API changed completely at 0.5.0: ignore older tutorials, start from `examples/sign.py` and `examples/read.py`. Signing needs an ES256 key and cert: a self-signed "bank CA" made with openssl is enough for a demo (shows as untrusted in public viewers). |
| c2patool (CLI, part of the [CAI open source tools](https://opensource.contentauthenticity.org/docs/introduction)) | Inspect and attach manifests from the shell | Free | 15 min | Fastest way to debug what a phone or OpenAI image carries. |
| [c2pa-js](https://github.com/contentauth/c2pa-js) | Read and validate in the browser, show a badge | Free | 2 to 3 h | Front-end badge for the onboarding page. |
| [Content Credentials Verify](https://contentcredentials.org/verify) | Public reference viewer | Free | 0 | Use live in the demo to show a file is recognized outside our app. |
| [AudioSeal](https://github.com/facebookresearch/audioseal), weights on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/facebook/audioseal) (`pip install audioseal`) | Embed and detect an inaudible mark with a 16-bit payload, sample-level localization, streaming models | Free, MIT | 1 h | `AudioSeal.load_detector("audioseal_detector_16bits")` then `detect_watermark(audio)`. CPU speed and survival through an 8 kHz phone codec unverified: test in the first 2 h. |
| [Video Seal](https://github.com/facebookresearch/videoseal) | Same for video frames | Free, MIT | 2 to 4 h | GPU likely needed for speed (unverified). Only if a video idea is chosen. |
| SynthID Text in [Transformers v4.46+](https://huggingface.co/blog/synthid-text) | Watermark text from an open model, Bayesian detector | Free | 3 to 4 h | Needs an open LLM and a detector trained on our keys. Low value for a bank demo. |
| [SynthID Detector portal](https://blog.google/technology/ai/google-synthid-ai-content-detector/) | Check Google/OpenAI/Nvidia/Kakao marks | Free, manual | 0 | No API, automation banned by its terms: show it by hand, never build on it. |
| A Pixel 10 (if a teammate has one) | Real hardware-signed capture, Assurance Level 2 | Hardware | 0 | Without one, simulate capture signing in a bank-controlled web capture page. |

Out of scope for C2PA but relevant to a French bank jury (not checked in this session, unverified): 2D-Doc signed barcodes on French official documents (tax notices, utility bills), PAdES/eIDAS signatures on PDFs, the EUDI wallet. A real payslip check rests on these, not on C2PA.

## Project ideas (2 or 3: one-line pitch, the demo moment the jury sees, the stack, the main risk)

1. **SignedCapture KYC: prove the genuine instead of detecting the fake.** Onboarding captures the selfie and ID photo in a bank-controlled page, signs them at capture with a C2PA manifest (session nonce, time, device hints) under the bank's own key, and a strict verifier checks the chain, rejects whole-file or broad exclusions (the Oct 2026 attack) and accepts only signers on the bank's own trust list.
   - Demo moment: (a) live selfie through the app: green badge, also recognized by Content Credentials Verify; (b) a ChatGPT or Nano Banana fake ID uploaded: "AI-generated, signed by OpenAI/Google" when its C2PA survives, else "no provenance: route to manual review"; (c) one pixel edited in the signed selfie: red "tampered"; (d) Buchanan's whole-file exclusion forgery: a naive verifier says valid, ours says rejected.
   - Stack: Next.js or plain HTML with getUserMedia, FastAPI + c2pa-python, openssl self-signed CA as "bank trust list", c2pa-js badge.
   - Main risk: provenance proves origin, not truth; a virtual camera feeding getUserMedia (injection attack, see [iProov case on MITRE ATLAS](https://idtechwire.com/iproov-red-team-attack-on-mobile-kyc-published-as-mitre-atlas-case-study/)) still gets signed, so the pitch must say which part needs device attestation (Pixel AL2, Play Integrity) in production.

2. **Bank Voice Seal: "is this really my advisor?"** The bank marks every outbound voice (advisor calls, voicebot, voice messages) with AudioSeal carrying an advisor ID + day in the 16 bits; the customer app checks a suspicious voice message or call recording in under a second, against the fake-advisor scam.
   - Demo moment: two voicemails "from your advisor", one real (marked), one voice clone; the app shows green and red, and the green one still passes after MP3 compression and, if it holds, after a phone-speaker re-recording.
   - Stack: Python + audioseal + FastAPI, a small web or mobile page to upload or record, any TTS for the clone.
   - Main risk: no mark does not prove a fake (live calls through the phone network may destroy it, unverified), and removal attacks exist; the value depends on customers using the check.

3. **Loan file provenance report.** For each uploaded payslip, invoice or ID: C2PA manifest and AI-generation assertions, SynthID hint (manual), PDF signature, metadata anomalies, one risk score per file.
   - Demo moment: a ChatGPT-made payslip flagged "created by OpenAI" from its C2PA, then the same file screenshotted: flagged only as "no provenance".
   - Stack: c2pa-python, pypdf, an LLM to write the analyst summary.
   - Main risk: weakest of the three: one screenshot strips everything ([fake document trends, Arya.ai 2026](https://arya.ai/blog/ai-document-fraud-detection-2026)), so it detects only lazy fraud.

## Verdict (2 or 3 lines)

Provenance is the most buildable and demoable angle of theme 02 (free pip libraries, a visible green/red moment), but on its own it cannot catch a fake: credentials are stripped, watermarks are removed, and even a Pixel signer was forged in 2026. Pitch "prove the genuine at capture, with a strict verifier" (idea 1, best fit for a bank jury), with idea 2 as the audio twin if AudioSeal survives the phone-codec test in the first two hours.
